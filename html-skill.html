📖 My Journey

I started learning HTML in 2023 — and honestly it was love at first tag. 💜 The first thing I ever typed was a simple <h1> heading and when I saw it appear in the browser, I was completely hooked. It felt like magic — writing words and watching them come to life on screen.

From there I moved on to links, images, and paragraphs. Then I discovered divs and how they let you structure a whole page. I learned about semantic HTML — using the right tags for the right content — and how it makes websites more accessible and better for search engines.

Today, HTML is second nature to me. Every project on this portfolio started with an HTML file. It's the skeleton of everything I build! 🦴

Links & Images

Attributes

id, class, style, href, src, alt, placeholder and more
